Every Dr Matt & Dr Mike video the HS2 Paper Sim knows about — 158 of them, 15 topics, 46 h 45 min end to end. Watch first, sit the mock papers after.

📺 Why this exists: inside the Paper Sim a video only appears once you have already got something wrong. That is good remediation and useless for orientation — and 73 of these 158 videos teach nothing a captured quiz question tests, so no amount of failing ever surfaces them. Here they are all in the open, grouped by topic. Tap a title to play it on this page.
What the numbers mean. The “explains N” tag counts Paper Sim questions whose tested fact this video actually states — matched from the video's own captions, not its title, judged from that text, gated on a verbatim quote re-found in the track, and the doubtful cases read again by hand — so every tag is a claim that was read, not guessed. The 73 videos with no tag are still good videos; they just cover ground none of the captured quizzes ask about. And these are Dr Matt & Dr Mike, not Hannetjie: where a video and her material disagree, she is the one being marked.
Videos and durations come straight from hs2-paper-m1's content/dmdm-all.json (the hs2-module1 shelf, flattened); the question counts from its content/video-matches.json. Topics are this page's own grouping. Watched ticks are stored in this browser only.
