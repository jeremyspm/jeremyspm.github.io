Casual Shifts Kit

Casual care work around a full-time BN at MIT Manukau: fewer applications, each in about three minutes.

The short version: stop sending 20-minute applications to random ads. Aim at about ten employers near Hillpark that hire casual caregivers all the time. Apply to each in about three minutes with the Paste kit, phone a few small rest homes, and let alerts bring you the rest.

Your weekends-and-weekday-daytimes availability, your car and your first aid certificate are exactly what aged care and home support are short of. The job is to say so at the top of every application.

Why 100 applications got nothing

  • The CV never said “care”. No target role, no availability, no dates, and referees with no roles listed. That’s fixed in the new CV.
  • Hospitals are the hardest door right now. Health NZ has run tight hiring controls since 2024 (RNZ, Aug 2026). Counties Manukau’s HCA ads asked for a Level 3–4 HCA qualification or experience, and the one Auckland pool for nursing students wants Year 2+ with an acute placement.
  • Casual care work mostly isn’t filled through one-off ads. Aged care hires through casual pools, facility managers and job alerts, and ads close as soon as someone good applies. Speed and a direct line beat a polished letter.
The plan

Build the kit once

New CV, your details, one generic cover-letter PDF, and ask Leigh to be your referee. The checklist is on the Tracker tab.

≈ 45 MIN, ONCE

Apply to the five “Do first” aged-care targets

CHT, Bupa, Oceania, Metlifecare and Summerset: every one is within a short drive of Hillpark. Open a target, tap Fill paste kit, copy, paste, submit.

≈ 3 MIN EACH

Phone three small homes

Weekdays 10–11:30am or 1:30–2:30pm, using the Phone script. Ask for the clinical or facility manager and offer to email your CV.

≈ 20 MIN

Set alerts, then apply within a day

SEEK saved searches plus each provider’s alerts. When one lands, the paste kit makes it a three-minute job. Skip ads that want a Level 3–4 qualification unless they also say training is provided.

≈ 10 MIN A WEEK

Follow up once, at five days

The Tracker flags anything that’s gone quiet and copies the follow-up for you. One follow-up, then move on.

≈ 2 MIN EACH

Why now, not December

Between applying and your first paid shift come the interview, police vetting, reference checks and orientation or buddy shifts, which often adds up to several weeks. Applications sent in October turn into summer shifts. Applications sent in December often start in February.

While you’re studying

  • You’re hired as a caregiver, not a student nurse. Do what that job description covers, even when your degree has taught you more.
  • Block exam weeks early. Tell your roster person your exam dates when you’re hired, not the week before.
  • Casual means you can say no. Take the weekend shifts that fit, and don’t let offered hours eat your study days.
